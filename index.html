📋 Assumptions & Notes

Plan B accommodation nights: Sunday night through Thursday night = 5 nights/week. All 3 months = 22 nights each (8–9 weekend nights removed per month).
Plan B train travel: agents take the train every Friday (Hualien→Taipei) and every Sunday (Taipei→Hualien). June: 4+4=8 one-way trips/person · July: 5+4=9 · August: 4+5=9 = 26 total one-way trips/person.
Plan B motorcycle: budgeted for accommodation nights only (22/month) — assumes bikes returned Friday morning and re-rented Sunday. If shop doesn't allow partial, moto cost is same as Plan A.
Meals are identical in both plans — NT$600/person/weekday × 66 workdays total. Weekend meals in Taipei are not budgeted (personal cost).
Plan B agent wellbeing benefit: agents spend weekends at home with family — may reduce fatigue and improve performance on a long 3-month deployment.
