📋 Plan B Assumptions

Accommodation: Sunday night through Thursday night = 5 nights/week. All 3 months = 22 Hualien nights each (8–9 Fri/Sat nights removed per month).
Train travel: every Friday (Hualien→Taipei) + every Sunday (Taipei→Hualien). June: 8 · July: 9 · August: 9 = 26 one-way trips per person total.
Motorcycle: budgeted for 22 accommodation nights/month (weekday use only). If shop requires full-month, cost = same as Plan A.
Meals remain identical to Plan A — NT$600/person/weekday, weekends not budgeted (personal cost while in Taipei).
Agent wellbeing: spending every weekend at home significantly reduces long-deployment fatigue — may benefit performance and retention.

📋 Comparison Notes

Accommodation savings = Plan A nights (92) minus Plan B nights (66) = 26 fewer hotel nights × 2 pax × nightly rate.
Meal costs are identical in both plans — same 66 weekdays, same NT$600/pax rate. Weekends not budgeted in either plan.
Travel cost is much higher in Plan B — 26 one-way trips vs 6 one-way trips per person. The extra train spend is the main cost of Plan B.
Motorcycle savings in Plan B = 22 days/month vs 30–31 days/month. If shop requires full-month rental, this saving disappears.
Plan B also provides a wellbeing benefit (weekly home time) which is not reflected in cost but matters for a 3-month deployment.
